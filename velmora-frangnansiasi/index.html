Expert advice

The art of wearing perfume.

Pulse points

Apply to your neck, wrists and behind the ears where body heat helps diffuse the scent all day.

Proper storage

Keep away from direct sunlight and extreme temperatures to preserve the delicate top notes.

Scent layering

Mix a drop of fresh citrus with a deeper woody scent to create your own signature blend.

Perfect timing

Apply right after a shower, when skin is warm and slightly moist, for the best lasting power.
