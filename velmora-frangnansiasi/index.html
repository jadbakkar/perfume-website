Our story

About us

VELMORA is more than a fragrance — it is a presence you feel and an impression that remains. A Lebanese fragrance house created to bring together luxury, quality, and distinction through thoughtfully priced scents. We carefully select European fragrance oils to create refined scents with distinctive character, elegant projection, and lasting performance. Every order is prepared, bottled, and packaged with care — creating a complete experience worthy of the fragrance inside.

Pulse points

Apply to your neck, wrists and behind the ears where body heat helps diffuse the scent all day.

Proper storage

Keep away from direct sunlight and extreme temperatures to preserve the top notes.

Scent layering

Mix a drop of fresh citrus with a deeper woody scent to create your own signature.

Best timing

Apply right after a shower when skin is warm and slightly moist for the best lasting power.
