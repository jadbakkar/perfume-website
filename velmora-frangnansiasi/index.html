Expert Advice

Fragrance Tips

Pulse Points

Apply pure oils to your neck, wrists, and behind the ears where body heat helps diffuse the fragrance all day.

Proper Storage

Keep your bottle away from direct sunlight and extreme temperatures to preserve the delicate top notes.

Scent Layering

Mix a drop of fresh citrus with a deeper woody scent to create your own signature blend effortlessly.

Application Timing

Apply fragrance right after a shower when your skin is warm and slightly moist for the best absorption and lasting sillage.
